The artist

Edward Mundondo is a DJ, music producer and digital artist who grew up between Zimbabwe, Botswana and South Africa and New York and New Jersey. Futurart is his practice of algorithmic media art, in the spirit of Refik Anadol’s machine-intelligence work, built from African landscapes, heritage and data.

How it’s made

The atlas uses only open tools and open data: Natural Earth maps, UNESCO, Wikidata, GBIF and iNaturalist records, and openly licensed photographs and film, each credited on screen. Particles flow over the land like wind. Each wonder is an emitter with its own motion: water cascades, mountains breathe mist, heritage sites orbit. When a tour opens, the swarm samples the real footage and becomes it.
